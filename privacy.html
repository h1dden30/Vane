Privacy policy

Last updated November 16, 2026

The short version

  • We don't sell your information, and we don't use it for advertising.
  • We keep only what we need to work your case, encrypted, and delete it 90 days after your last re-check.
  • If your employer pays for Vane, they only see whether your case is open or complete, never what we find.
  • You can ask us to delete anything we hold about you at any time.

Who we are

Vane is operated by [LLC NAME], a Michigan limited liability company. You can reach us at hello@getvane.com or by mail at [MAILING ADDRESS]. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean [LLC NAME].

What we collect and why

When you visit this website. Our web server keeps basic technical logs, like IP address, browser type and the pages requested, for up to [NUMBER] days so we can keep the site running and secure. We don't use ad trackers, third-party analytics or advertising cookies.

When you contact us. We keep what you put in the contact form or email us, such as a name and a way to reach you, so we can reply. You can use a nickname.

When you create an account and pay. We keep your email address, your plan and your billing history. Payments are handled by [PAYMENT PROCESSOR]. We never see or store your full card number.

When you use the Vane app. The details you search for, such as your name, past addresses and phone numbers, and the screenshots of your listings stay on your device. Our servers receive only what's needed to run your plan: [DESCRIBE, for example the list of sites where a listing was found and the date it was removed, without your personal details].

When you open a Rapid Response case. To find and remove your listings, we hold the details you give us at intake (names, addresses, phone numbers, usernames) and what we find, including screenshots. These files are encrypted, available only to the person working your case, and deleted 90 days after your last re-check unless you choose a monitoring plan. We confirm your identity on a video call and do not keep a copy of your ID. Your report is sent through a link that expires after 7 days.

Software and AI tools. We use software, including AI tools, to organize findings and draft removal requests. These tools run on systems we control, or are provided by vendors whose contracts do not allow them to train on or keep your information. A person reviews every result.

When you book a one-on-one session. Our team only takes the notes needed to finish your removals. We delete session notes [NUMBER] days after your case is closed, unless you ask us to keep them.

Removal requests we send for you

Some sites require a request from you or from someone acting for you. We only send a request on your behalf after you give us permission, and we only share the minimum information that site needs to find and remove your listing.

Healthcare and other organizations

When an employer pays for Vane, they can see how many employees have enrolled and whether a case is open or complete. They can't see who found what, which sites listed you, or anything else about your results. We will not share your results with your employer unless you tell us to in writing.

Who we share information with

We share information only with service providers that help us run Vane, such as our payment processor, email provider, hosting provider and the AI tools described above. They can only use it to provide their service to us. We don't sell or rent personal information, and we don't share it with data brokers or advertisers.

Legal requests

We will only disclose information if we're legally required to, such as by a valid court order. When the law allows, we'll tell you before we do. Because we delete case files on a fixed schedule, there is often very little for us to hand over.

How long we keep information

We keep account and billing records while your plan is active and for as long as tax and accounting rules require after that. Everything else is deleted when it's no longer needed for the purposes above.

Your choices

Michigan doesn't currently have a comprehensive consumer privacy law, but we give everyone the same rights anyway. You can ask us to:

  • tell you what information we hold about you
  • correct it
  • delete it

Email hello@getvane.com and we'll respond within 30 days. Deleting your account doesn't remove the files stored on your own device. You can delete those yourself in the app.

Security

We protect the information we hold with encryption, access controls and limited staff access. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal information, we'll notify you without unreasonable delay, as Michigan's Identity Theft Protection Act requires.

Children

Vane is for adults. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18 without a parent or guardian.

Changes to this policy

If we make a significant change, we'll update the date at the top and email account holders before it takes effect.
