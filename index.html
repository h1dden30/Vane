Questions

Why not just use Google's free "Results about you" tool?

Turn it on too. But it only hides links in Google. The listing is still on the site that sells it, where anyone can find it directly. We get it removed at the source.

How is this different from DeleteMe or Incogni?

They run on autopilot. Vane keeps checking on a schedule too, and adds what automation can't: a person who starts within one business day when you're threatened, a look at your social media and public records, and a written report.

Can you guarantee no one finds me?

No, and be careful with anyone who does. Some records, like property records, are public by law. We remove what can be removed, tell you what's left, and keep checking.

What does my employer see?

Only whether your case is open or complete. Never what we found.

I think my phone is being watched. Is it safe to contact you?

Use a device the other person can't access, like a friend's phone or a library computer, or ask your advocate to contact us for you.

How do you handle my information?

Only what we need for your case, encrypted, deleted 90 days after your last re-check. We don't keep a copy of your ID. Your bank statement just says VANE.
