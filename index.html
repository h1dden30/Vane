How it works

1. Search

We check the people-search sites most often used to find someone and show you what each one has.

2. Document

Each listing gets a dated screenshot before and after removal, saved where only you can see it.

3. Remove

We walk you through each removal, or do it with you. Some sites make this hard on purpose, so a person finishes what the software can't.

4. Re-check

Listings often come back after a few months. We check again on a schedule and let you know.

Vane and Michigan's ACP

The state's Address Confidentiality Program keeps your real address out of public records. It doesn't cover the data brokers and people-search sites that already have it. That's the part we handle.

Your information stays with you

The search and your screenshots run on your own device. We don't keep a copy of your address, your ID or your listings on our servers, so there's nothing of yours for us to lose.

What we can't do

  • Remove you from the internet permanently. Nobody can, which is why we keep checking.
  • Take down court records. They're public, but we'll explain your options.
  • Cover every site. We publish the list of sites we check.

Common questions

How is this different from DeleteMe or Incogni?

You get screenshots showing each listing before and after it's removed, your information stays on your device, and a person steps in when a site blocks the software. It also costs less.

Can you guarantee my information won't come back?

No, and you should be careful with anyone who does. Data brokers buy new data all the time, so listings can reappear. That's why we re-check every 3 months and remove them again.

I think my phone or computer is being monitored. Is it safe to sign up?

Use a device the other person can't access, like a library computer or a friend's phone, or ask your advocate to contact us for you. We can check your own device for tracking apps during a session.

What shows up on my bank statement?

A neutral name, VANE, with no mention of privacy or safety.

Does this replace a protective order or my advocate?

No. Vane handles one technical piece of staying safe. Your advocate, the courts and Michigan's ACP handle the rest, and we work with all of them.

How does the healthcare plan work?

Your organization pays per enrolled employee. Each employee signs up privately, and we never share what we find about them with their employer.
